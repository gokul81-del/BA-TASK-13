## Objective
To analyze retail sales data, identify business insights, and create an interactive dashboard using Tableau Public.

## Dataset
- **File:** `tableau_retail_business_dataset.csv`
- **Records:** 850
- **Columns:** 11
- **Data:** Sales, Profit, Region, Category, Product, Customer Segment, Quantity, and Discount.

## Visualizations
1. Heat Map – Sales by Category and Region
2. Line Chart – Monthly Sales Trend
3. Pie Chart – Sales by Customer Segment
4. Scatter Plot – Sales vs Profit
5. Histogram – Order Quantity Distribution
6. Bar Chart – Profit by Category

## Dashboard Features
- Interactive filters for Region, Category, and Order Date.
- Comparison of sales and profit.
- Identification of business trends and patterns.

## Key Findings
The dashboard helps identify high-performing categories, regional sales patterns, customer segment contributions, and product profitability.

## Conclusion
This project demonstrates how Tableau Public can be used to explore retail data and present business insights through interactive visualizations.

## Recommendations
- Improve the profitability of underperforming categories.
- Analyze regional sales differences.
- Use sales trends to support inventory planning.
- Monitor discounts and profit margins.
